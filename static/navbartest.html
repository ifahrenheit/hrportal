What this test page is showing you

  • Reclaimed width. The grid above runs full-bleed. Under the sidebar you were losing roughly 240px on every wide report.
  • Five triggers, not twenty-five links. The top bar maps 1:1 to your perm_groups, so the Jinja gates port over almost unchanged.
  • The palette indexes itself. Ctrl K scans every rendered .mega-item at load. Add a permission-gated link and it becomes searchable automatically, and stays hidden for users who cannot see it. No second list to maintain.
  • The sub-nav absorbs growth. New pages join a section's tab row instead of lengthening the top bar. This is the piece that makes the pattern survive your next ten modules.
  • Empty sections collapse. A trigger whose items are all gated out should not render. Wrap each <button class="navbtn"> in the same or condition you use for the section gate today.
