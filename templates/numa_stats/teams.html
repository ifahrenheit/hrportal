Looker vs the Agents page
Looker counts per team (each conversation once, CSAT and SLA from every survey and message). The Agents page adds up each agent's own row: conversations are summed (a conversation two agents touched counts twice), CSAT is each agent's WhatsApp CSAT weighted by their conversations, and email SLA is the agents' median. Team membership also differs: Looker uses Numa's team attribute, the Agents page uses our GX / Night Shift split.
