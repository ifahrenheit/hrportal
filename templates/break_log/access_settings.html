Choose per account and group who can use Break Log. Changes apply right away, and nobody has to log out. Admins, supervisors and sub-admins with the Overbreak permission can always monitor. Groups that aren't listed here yet, or are marked New, have everything off.

  • Log breaks: can open Break Log and clock their own breaks. Their breaks show in Break Monitoring.
  • Monitor: can open Break Monitoring and Overbreak Review.
  • Overbreak Review: their overbreaks show in the portal's Overbreak Review, where supervisors create or waive IRs. Set the from date to start on a given day; leave it blank for "always". This does not turn off the overbreak sheet: groups in the sheet (GYG) still get their automatic IRs. Overbreak Review shows the IR already filed for that agent and date, so the reviewer waives it ("Covered by IR-…") instead of filing a second one.
